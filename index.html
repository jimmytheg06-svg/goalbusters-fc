Our Story

Our History

FC Goalbusters Nepali Club was founded in 2012 with a deep love for soccer and a vision to bring the rich values and traditions of the Nepali community to the heart of Dallas, Texas. The club was born out of a shared passion for the beautiful game, as well as a desire to create a space where people could come together, celebrate their heritage, and build connections through soccer.

From its humble beginnings, the club's mission was simple: to unite Nepali individuals and families in Dallas through a common love for soccer, while fostering a sense of belonging and community. Over the years, FC Goalbusters has grown into a respected club that not only excels in competitive soccer but also promotes the cultural values of respect, unity, and family—values that are at the core of Nepali traditions.

Our early days were spent organizing informal matches and small community events. But as word spread, the club began attracting a wider range of players, from youth to adults, who shared the same passion for soccer and the desire to strengthen the Nepali presence in the Dallas area. We became more than just a soccer team; we became a family—a place where players, coaches, and supporters could bond, learn, and grow together.

Through the years, we've participated in local tournaments, organized community events, and established strong partnerships with various cultural and sports organizations in Dallas. Our focus has always been on promoting sportsmanship, teamwork, and respect, both on the field and in everyday life.

FC Goalbusters is committed to providing an environment where young athletes can learn not only the technical skills of soccer but also the values of discipline, leadership, and perseverance. The club aims to continue building bridges between the Nepali community and the broader Dallas community, celebrating our culture and identity while working together to achieve excellence in soccer.

As we look ahead to the future, we remain dedicated to the vision of fostering a positive, inclusive environment where players can reach their full potential, make lifelong friendships, and carry the pride of Nepali heritage with them on and off the field.

Who We Are

A Club Built on Community


Goal Busters FC was founded in 2012 by a group of players who simply wanted to enjoy the beautiful game together. Over 14 years we've grown into one of DFW's most beloved community clubs — open to all backgrounds, all skill levels, no pressure.

We're not here for contracts. We're here for the love of football, the friendships forged on the pitch, and the shared spirit that makes every match worth showing up for. There is a spot for you here.
